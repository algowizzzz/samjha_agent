# Summary

The Capital Adequacy Requirements (CAR) Guideline 2024, effective January 2024, is issued by the Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions (OSFI) for Canadian banks. This comprehensive guideline covers risk-based capital requirements, capital definitions, and operational frameworks. It includes nine chapters addressing credit risk (standardized and internal ratings-based approaches), operational risk, securitization, settlement, counterparty risk, CVA risk, and market risk. The document provides Basel III implementation guidance, reporting requirements through Basel Capital Adequacy Reporting (BCAR), and detailed instructions for Internal Ratings-Based (IRB) institutions, including collateral management, risk quantification, and validation procedures.
