**Document Type:** Guideline

**Rationale:** This is a regulatory guideline issued by OSFI (Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions) establishing capital adequacy requirements for banks. It provides comprehensive frameworks, definitions, and methodologies across nine chapters covering risk-based capital requirements, rather than prescribing step-by-step procedures or operational policies. The document explicitly labels itself as a "Guideline" and serves as the authoritative standard for Basel III capital compliance in the banking sector.
