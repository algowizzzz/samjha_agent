## Analysis

### 1. Category
**Compliance Review & Document Processing**

This task involves extracting requirements from documentation and performing a gap analysis, which falls under compliance and regulatory document processing.

---

### 2. Key Requirements

- **Extract MUST requirements**: Identify and isolate all mandatory requirements from the source document(s)
- **Extract SHOULD requirements**: Identify and isolate all recommended/desired requirements from the source document(s)
- **Generate gap summary**: Analyze and document the differences, missing elements, or discrepancies between MUST and SHOULD requirements

---

### 3. Complexity Assessment

**Rating: Medium**

**Rationale:**
- Requires understanding of requirement classification standards (MUST vs. SHOULD terminology)
- Involves multiple steps: extraction, categorization, and comparative analysis
- Moderate scope with clear deliverables
- Does not appear to involve complex business logic or multiple system integrations
- Straightforward if source documents are well-structured; complexity increases if documents are unstructured or ambiguous

---

### 4. Dependencies

- **Source documents**: The actual requirement documents or specifications to be analyzed
- **Definition clarity**: Clear definitions of what constitutes MUST vs. SHOULD requirements for this customer
- **Format specification**: Desired format/template for the gap summary output
- **Domain context**: Understanding of Contoso's business domain and regulatory requirements
- **Reference standards**: Any industry standards or frameworks (e.g., ISO, regulatory compliance) that apply to the requirement classification
