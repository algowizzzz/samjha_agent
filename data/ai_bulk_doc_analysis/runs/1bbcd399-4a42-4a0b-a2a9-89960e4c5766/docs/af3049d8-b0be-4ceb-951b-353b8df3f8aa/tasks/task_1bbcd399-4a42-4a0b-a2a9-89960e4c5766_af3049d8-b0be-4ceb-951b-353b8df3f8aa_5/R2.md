# Actionable Recommendations for Ticket TCK-006

## 1. Recommended Approach

1. **Request and Obtain the Policy Document**
   - Contact Litware to confirm the policy document location and format (PDF, Word, text file, etc.)
   - Ensure you have access to the complete, current version of the document
   - Verify document readability and that it's not corrupted or password-protected

2. **Clarify Extraction Scope and Criteria**
   - Schedule a brief call or send written clarification to Litware defining what constitutes a "key requirement"
   - Confirm which sections of the policy document are in scope (entire document vs. specific chapters/sections)
   - Establish the expected volume of requirements to extract

3. **Define Output Format Requirements**
   - Confirm the desired output format with Litware (bulleted list, table, structured document, database format, etc.)
   - Determine if requirements should be categorized, prioritized, or cross-referenced
   - Clarify any specific naming conventions or metadata to include (e.g., requirement ID, source section, applicability)

4. **Conduct Initial Document Review**
   - Perform a preliminary scan of the policy document to understand its structure and complexity
   - Identify potential ambiguities or areas requiring subject matter expert input
   - Estimate the actual effort required based on document length and complexity

5. **Extract and Organize Requirements**
   - Systematically identify and extract all requirements meeting the defined criteria
   - Organize requirements in the specified format
   - Cross-reference each requirement to its source location in the policy document

6. **Quality Assurance and Validation**
   - Review extracted requirements for accuracy and completeness
   - Verify that no requirements were missed
   - Validate extracted requirements with a subject matter expert if available

7. **Deliver and Obtain Approval**
   - Present extracted requirements to Litware in the agreed-upon format
   - Request feedback and make any necessary revisions
   - Document the final deliverable and close the ticket

## 3. Risk Factors

### High-Impact Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|-----------|
| **Unclear Requirement Definition** | High | Medium | Obtain explicit clarification from Litware before starting extraction; define criteria in writing |
| **Document Accessibility Issues** | Medium | High | Confirm document format and access early; test readability immediately |
| **Scope Creep** | Medium | Medium | Establish clear boundaries upfront; document agreed-upon scope in writing |

### Medium-Impact Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|-----------|
| **Ambiguous Policy Language** | Medium | Medium | Flag ambiguous sections; escalate to SME for interpretation; document assumptions |
| **Missing Requirements** | Medium | Medium | Use systematic review process; cross-check against document structure; peer review |
| **Format Incompatibility** | Low | Medium | Confirm output format compatibility early; test with sample data |
| **Document Length Underestimation** | Medium | Low | Conduct preliminary review to estimate actual scope; adjust timeline if needed |

### Low-Impact Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|-----------|
| **Minor Formatting Issues** | Low | Low | Use standard formatting tools; apply quality checks before delivery |
| **Communication Delays** | Low | Low | Set clear response time expectations; use multiple communication channels if needed |
