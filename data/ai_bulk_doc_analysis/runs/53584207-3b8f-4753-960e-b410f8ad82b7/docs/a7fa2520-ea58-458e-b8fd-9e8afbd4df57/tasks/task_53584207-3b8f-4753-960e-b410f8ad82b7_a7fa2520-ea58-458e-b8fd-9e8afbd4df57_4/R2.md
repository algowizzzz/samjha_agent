# Actionable Recommendations for Ticket TCK-005

## 1. Recommended Approach

1. **Clarify Compliance Scope**
   - Contact Woodgrove to identify which regulatory frameworks apply (GDPR, HIPAA, SOX, industry-specific standards, etc.)
   - Confirm the specific compliance domains relevant to their business operations
   - Document the scope in writing to align expectations

2. **Gather Required Documentation**
   - Request complete collection of documents requiring compliance review
   - Obtain Woodgrove's internal compliance policies and procedures
   - Collect any existing compliance audit criteria or checklists
   - Verify document formats and accessibility

3. **Establish Evaluation Criteria**
   - Create or obtain a detailed compliance requirements checklist
   - Map applicable regulatory standards to specific document sections
   - Define what constitutes a compliance gap, violation, or risk
   - Establish severity levels for identified issues

4. **Conduct Document Analysis**
   - Systematically review each document against compliance criteria
   - Document findings with specific references to regulatory requirements
   - Categorize issues by severity (Critical/High/Medium/Low)
   - Cross-reference violations against multiple applicable standards

5. **Identify Gaps and Violations**
   - Compile comprehensive list of non-compliance areas
   - Assess business impact of each identified issue
   - Determine root causes where applicable
   - Flag any high-risk or critical compliance gaps

6. **Develop Remediation Recommendations**
   - Provide specific, actionable recommendations for each finding
   - Prioritize recommendations by compliance risk and implementation effort
   - Include estimated effort for remediation activities
   - Suggest timeline for addressing critical issues

7. **Prepare Compliance Assessment Report**
   - Executive summary with key findings and risk assessment
   - Detailed findings with evidence and regulatory references
   - Prioritized recommendations with implementation guidance
   - Appendices with supporting documentation and checklists

8. **Present Findings to Woodgrove**
   - Schedule review meeting with relevant stakeholders
   - Walk through findings and recommendations
   - Address questions and clarify next steps
   - Obtain sign-off on report and recommendations
