# Actionable Recommendations for Ticket TCK-006

## 1. Recommended Approach

1. **Request Missing Documentation**
   - Contact Litware to obtain the policy document in its original format (PDF, Word, etc.)
   - Confirm the document is complete and current
   - Request any supplementary materials that provide context

2. **Clarify Extraction Criteria**
   - Schedule a brief call or send a detailed questionnaire to Litware
   - Define what constitutes "key requirements" in their specific context
   - Identify any prioritization or categorization scheme they prefer
   - Determine scope boundaries (e.g., all requirements vs. top-level only)

3. **Establish Output Specifications**
   - Confirm preferred delivery format (bulleted list, table, structured document, etc.)
   - Determine required level of detail for each extracted requirement
   - Identify any specific templates or standards to follow
   - Clarify if cross-references or citations to source document are needed

4. **Conduct Document Review**
   - Perform initial scan of the policy document to understand structure and scope
   - Identify document sections, headings, and key terminology
   - Note any ambiguous or complex requirements that may need clarification

5. **Extract and Organize Requirements**
   - Systematically extract all identified key requirements
   - Organize by logical categories or document sections
   - Maintain traceability to source locations within the policy document

6. **Quality Assurance**
   - Review extracted requirements for accuracy and completeness
   - Verify consistency in terminology and formatting
   - Cross-check against original document to ensure no critical items were missed

7. **Obtain Validation**
   - Submit extracted requirements to Litware's designated approval authority
   - Incorporate feedback and revisions as needed
   - Finalize and deliver approved requirements document

## 3. Risk Factors

### High-Impact Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|-----------|
| **Missing or Incomplete Document** | Medium | High | Request document upfront with confirmation of completeness; establish deadline for receipt |
| **Ambiguous Requirement Definition** | High | High | Conduct discovery call early to align on criteria; document agreed-upon definition in writing |
| **Scope Creep** | Medium | Medium | Clearly define boundaries in initial communication; document what is/isn't included |

### Medium-Impact Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|-----------|
| **Multiple Revision Cycles** | Medium | Medium | Establish clear approval process upfront; limit revision rounds (e.g., max 2) |
| **Domain Knowledge Gap** | Medium | Medium | Identify need for SME early; allocate budget for expert consultation if needed |
| **Format Incompatibility** | Low | Medium | Confirm document format is readable; have conversion tools available |
| **Unclear Requirement Prioritization** | Medium | Low | Ask customer to prioritize requirements or indicate relative importance |

### Low-Impact Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|-----------|
| **Terminology Inconsistencies** | Low | Low | Maintain glossary of terms; flag ambiguities for customer clarification |
| **Extraction Tool Limitations** | Low | Low | Use combination of automated and manual extraction methods |
