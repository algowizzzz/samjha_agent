# Actionable Recommendations for Ticket TCK-006

## 1. Recommended Approach

1. **Request Missing Documentation**
   - Contact Litware to obtain the policy document in its original format (PDF, Word, etc.)
   - Confirm receipt and readability of the document

2. **Clarify Extraction Criteria**
   - Request definition of "key requirements" (e.g., mandatory obligations, compliance items, operational procedures)
   - Ask for any specific domains or sections to prioritize if the document is lengthy
   - Confirm preferred output format (bulleted list, table, structured document, etc.)

3. **Conduct Initial Document Review**
   - Perform a preliminary scan of the policy document to understand its structure and scope
   - Identify major sections and requirement categories
   - Flag any ambiguous or complex language requiring clarification

4. **Extract and Organize Requirements**
   - Systematically extract requirements from each relevant section
   - Categorize requirements by type (mandatory, recommended, conditional, etc.)
   - Cross-reference related requirements to ensure completeness

5. **Format and Structure Output**
   - Organize extracted requirements in the agreed-upon format
   - Add context or source references (section numbers, page numbers) for traceability
   - Create a summary document with an overview of total requirements identified

6. **Quality Assurance Review**
   - Verify completeness against the original document
   - Validate accuracy of extracted language
   - Ensure consistency in formatting and terminology

7. **Deliver and Obtain Feedback**
   - Provide the extracted requirements to Litware
   - Request confirmation that all key requirements have been captured
   - Incorporate any feedback or corrections
