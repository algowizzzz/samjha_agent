# FINAL AGGREGATED DOCUMENT

## Document Summary

This OSFI guideline (effective January 2024) establishes capital adequacy requirements for Canadian banks under Basel III framework. It covers risk-based capital definitions, operational and credit risk approaches, securitization, counterparty risk, market risk, and CVA risk. Includes implementation notes for IRB institutions and reporting manuals.

## Document Type

**Guideline**

This is a regulatory guideline issued by OSFI (Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions) establishing mandatory capital adequacy requirements for banks. It provides comprehensive frameworks, definitions, and methodologies across nine chapters covering risk-based capital requirements, rather than prescribing step-by-step procedures or organizational policies.
