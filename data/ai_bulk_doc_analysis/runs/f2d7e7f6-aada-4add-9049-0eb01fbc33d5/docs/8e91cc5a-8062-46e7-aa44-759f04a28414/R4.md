## Summary

This OSFI guideline (effective January 2024) establishes capital adequacy requirements for Canadian banks under Basel III framework. It covers risk-based capital requirements, capital definitions, operational and credit risk assessment, securitization, market risk, and counterparty risk. Includes implementation notes for Internal Ratings-Based institutions and reporting procedures.
