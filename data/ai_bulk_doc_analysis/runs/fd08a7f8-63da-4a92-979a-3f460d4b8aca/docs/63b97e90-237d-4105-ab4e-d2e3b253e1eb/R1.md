# Summary of Capital Adequacy Requirements (CAR) Guideline 2024

The Capital Adequacy Requirements (CAR) Guideline 2024, effective January 2024, is issued by Canada's Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions (OSFI). This comprehensive regulatory framework establishes risk-based capital requirements for Canadian banks across nine key areas: risk overview, capital definition, operational risk, credit risk (standardized and internal ratings-based approaches), securitization, settlement and counterparty risk, credit valuation adjustment risk, and market risk. The guideline incorporates Basel III reforms and provides implementation guidance for Internal Ratings-Based (IRB) institutions, addressing collateral management, risk quantification, rating validation, and data maintenance standards.
