# Actionable Recommendations for Ticket TCK-006

## 1. Recommended Approach

1. **Request Missing Information from Customer**
   - Contact Litware to obtain the policy document (confirm file format: PDF, Word, etc.)
   - Ask for clarification on what constitutes "key requirements" (mandatory vs. optional, regulatory vs. operational, etc.)
   - Confirm desired output format (bulleted list, structured table, categorized breakdown, etc.)
   - Define scope boundaries (include appendices, footnotes, related documents?)

2. **Prepare Extraction Framework**
   - Develop or select a template for organizing extracted requirements
   - Create a checklist of criteria for identifying "key" requirements based on customer feedback
   - Identify any domain-specific terminology or regulatory context relevant to Litware's industry

3. **Conduct Document Review**
   - Perform initial scan of the policy document to understand structure and content
   - Identify sections containing requirements vs. supporting material
   - Flag any ambiguous or interconnected requirements for special attention

4. **Extract and Categorize Requirements**
   - Extract requirements according to defined criteria
   - Organize by category, priority, or functional area as appropriate
   - Document source location (page number, section) for each requirement

5. **Quality Assurance & Validation**
   - Cross-reference extracted requirements against source document
   - Verify completeness and accuracy
   - Obtain customer review/approval if SME validation is needed

6. **Deliver Structured Output**
   - Format requirements in agreed-upon format
   - Provide summary document with extraction methodology
   - Include any assumptions or clarifications made during extraction

## 2. Estimated Effort

**Effort Level: MEDIUM**

**Justification:**
- **Information Gathering Phase**: 30-45 minutes (clarifying requirements and obtaining document)
- **Document Analysis & Extraction**: 2-3 hours (depends on document length and complexity)
- **Organization & Formatting**: 45-60 minutes
- **Quality Review & Validation**: 30-45 minutes
- **Total Estimated Time**: 4-5 hours of professional effort

**Resource Requirements:**
- 1 Business Analyst or Document Specialist
- Potentially 1 Subject Matter Expert for validation (0.5-1 hour)
- Standard document processing tools (PDF reader, spreadsheet/document editor)

## 3. Risk Factors

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|-----------|
| **Missing Policy Document** | Medium | High | Request document immediately; set clear deadline for receipt |
| **Ambiguous "Key Requirements" Definition** | High | Medium | Provide examples and ask for specific criteria upfront |
| **Overly Long/Complex Document** | Medium | Medium | Negotiate scope; consider phased approach if document exceeds 50+ pages |
| **Conflicting or Unclear Requirements** | Medium | Medium | Document conflicts; flag for customer clarification |
| **Scope Creep** | Medium | Medium | Define boundaries clearly; document out-of-scope items separately |
| **Lack of Domain Knowledge** | Low | Medium | Identify if SME consultation is needed; request customer guidance |
| **Output Format Mismatch** | Medium | Low | Confirm format preferences before starting extraction work |
