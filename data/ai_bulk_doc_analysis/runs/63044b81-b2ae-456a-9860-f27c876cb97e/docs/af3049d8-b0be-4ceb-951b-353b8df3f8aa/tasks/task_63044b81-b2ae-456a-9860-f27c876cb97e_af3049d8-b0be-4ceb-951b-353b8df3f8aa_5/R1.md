## Analysis

### 1. Category
**Document Processing & Data Extraction**

This task involves reviewing a policy document and identifying and extracting key requirements from it.

---

### 2. Key Requirements

- Extract key requirements from a policy document
- Identify and isolate the most important/relevant requirements from the source material
- Deliver extracted requirements in a structured, usable format

---

### 3. Complexity Assessment

**Complexity Level: Medium**

**Rationale:**
- Requires domain knowledge to identify what constitutes "key" requirements versus supporting details
- Policy documents typically contain complex language and interconnected concepts that need careful analysis
- The task is straightforward in scope but requires careful attention to detail and understanding of policy context
- No indication of multiple documents or cross-referencing needs, which keeps it from being High complexity

---

### 4. Dependencies

- **Policy Document**: The actual policy document file (format: PDF, Word, etc.) needs to be provided
- **Definition of "Key"**: Clarification on what criteria determine which requirements are "key" (e.g., mandatory vs. optional, regulatory vs. operational)
- **Output Format**: Specification of desired output format (e.g., bulleted list, structured table, categorized sections)
- **Subject Matter Expert**: Potentially needed to validate extracted requirements for accuracy and completeness
- **Scope Boundaries**: Clarification on whether requirements from appendices, footnotes, or related documents should be included
