# Actionable Recommendations for Ticket TCK-006

## 1. Recommended Approach

1. **Request Missing Documentation**
   - Contact Litware to obtain the policy document in its original format (PDF, Word, etc.)
   - Confirm the document file name, version, and any relevant metadata

2. **Clarify Extraction Criteria**
   - Request specific guidance on what constitutes "key requirements" for their use case
   - Ask for examples of requirements they consider critical vs. supplementary
   - Determine if there are specific policy sections or categories to prioritize

3. **Define Output Format**
   - Confirm desired output format (bulleted list, structured table, categorized document, etc.)
   - Establish any required metadata (requirement ID, category, priority level, etc.)
   - Determine if cross-referencing to original document sections is needed

4. **Conduct Document Review**
   - Perform initial scan of the policy document to understand structure and scope
   - Identify all potential requirement statements and their locations
   - Flag any ambiguous or overlapping requirements for clarification

5. **Extract and Organize Requirements**
   - Systematically extract identified key requirements
   - Organize by logical categories or policy sections
   - Ensure each requirement is clearly stated and actionable

6. **Format and Present Results**
   - Structure extracted requirements according to agreed-upon format
   - Include source references (section numbers, page numbers) for traceability
   - Create a clean, professional deliverable document

7. **Obtain Customer Validation**
   - Submit extracted requirements to Litware for review and approval
   - Incorporate feedback and revisions as needed
   - Finalize and deliver approved requirements document

## 3. Risk Factors

### High-Risk Factors

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-----------|
| **Missing/Unavailable Document** | Project blocker; cannot proceed | Establish document delivery deadline upfront; escalate if not received within 48 hours |
| **Ambiguous "Key Requirements" Definition** | Extracted requirements may not meet customer expectations; rework required | Request explicit criteria and examples before extraction begins |
| **Subjective Interpretation Disagreement** | Customer disputes what qualifies as "key"; multiple revision cycles | Document decision rationale; present options for customer selection |

### Medium-Risk Factors

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-----------|
| **Large/Complex Policy Document** | Extraction effort significantly increases | Conduct initial scope assessment; request prioritization if document exceeds 50+ pages |
| **Multiple Revision Cycles** | Timeline extension; resource drain | Limit revision rounds (e.g., max 2 iterations); clarify requirements upfront |
| **Format Compatibility Issues** | Difficulty extracting from scanned/image-based PDFs | Request editable document format; plan for manual extraction if needed |

### Low-Risk Factors

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-----------|
| **Minor Formatting Adjustments** | Minimal rework | Use flexible templates; build formatting time into estimate |
| **Typos/Grammar in Source Document** | Potential confusion in extracted requirements | Standardize language during extraction; flag unclear passages |
