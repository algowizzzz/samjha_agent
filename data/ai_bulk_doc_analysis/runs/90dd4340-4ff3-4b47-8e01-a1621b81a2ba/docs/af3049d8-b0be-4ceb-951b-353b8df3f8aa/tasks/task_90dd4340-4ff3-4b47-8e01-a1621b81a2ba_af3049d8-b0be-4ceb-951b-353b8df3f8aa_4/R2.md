# Actionable Recommendations for Ticket TCK-005

## 1. Recommended Approach

1. **Initiate Customer Contact**
   - Reach out to Woodgrove immediately to clarify scope and requirements
   - Schedule a brief discovery call or send detailed intake questionnaire

2. **Define Compliance Framework**
   - Identify applicable regulatory standards (GDPR, HIPAA, SOX, industry-specific, etc.)
   - Document the specific compliance requirements and evaluation criteria
   - Obtain or reference the compliance checklist to be used for assessment

3. **Gather Document Specifications**
   - Request all documents requiring analysis from the customer
   - Clarify document types, formats, volume, and any sensitive data handling requirements
   - Establish secure transfer mechanism if handling confidential/regulated data

4. **Establish Evaluation Criteria**
   - Create or confirm the compliance assessment framework
   - Define what constitutes a "gap," "risk," or "non-compliance" finding
   - Identify severity levels for any issues discovered

5. **Conduct Document Review**
   - Perform systematic analysis of all provided documents
   - Cross-reference against identified compliance standards
   - Document findings with specific evidence and locations

6. **Generate Compliance Report**
   - Compile findings into structured report format
   - Include executive summary, detailed findings, risk ratings, and recommendations
   - Provide remediation guidance for identified gaps

7. **Stakeholder Validation**
   - Present findings to Woodgrove's compliance team or subject matter experts
   - Address questions and refine recommendations based on feedback
   - Obtain sign-off on final report

---

## 2. Estimated Effort

**Medium to High** (8-16 business hours)

### Justification:

- **Discovery & Clarification**: 2-3 hours (customer communication, requirement gathering)
- **Framework Definition**: 1-2 hours (regulatory research and criteria establishment)
- **Document Review**: 3-8 hours (depends heavily on document volume and complexity)
- **Report Generation**: 2-3 hours (analysis synthesis and documentation)
- **Stakeholder Review & Revision**: 1-2 hours (feedback incorporation)

**Variables affecting effort:**
- Number and length of documents to review
- Complexity of applicable regulatory frameworks
- Availability of customer for clarification questions
- Specificity of compliance criteria provided

## 3. Risk Factors

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation Strategy |
|------|----------|-------------------|
| **Unclear Scope** | High | Conduct immediate discovery call; document all requirements in writing before proceeding |
| **Missing Regulatory Context** | High | Request explicit list of applicable frameworks; research industry standards if needed |
| **Incomplete Documentation** | Medium | Establish clear document submission deadline; confirm receipt of all materials before analysis begins |
| **Ambiguous Compliance Criteria** | Medium | Create detailed evaluation checklist with customer approval before analysis phase |
| **Subject Matter Expertise Gap** | Medium | Identify internal compliance SME or external consultant availability; escalate if needed |
| **Timeline Pressure** | Medium | Confirm realistic deadline with customer; establish interim milestone check-ins |
| **Data Sensitivity/Security** | Medium | Implement secure handling protocols; confirm data classification and confidentiality requirements |
| **Stakeholder Unavailability** | Low | Schedule validation meeting early; establish backup reviewers |
