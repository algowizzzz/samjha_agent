AI Document Review Tool Policy
Version 1.0
Effective Date: Nov. 14, 2025
1. INTRODUCTION
This policy aims to ensure safe, compliant, and effective deployment of AI-powered document review
tools. It covers organizational standards, staff responsibilities, and continuous improvement measures.
2. DEFINITIONS
AI Document Review Tool: Automated system for document review.
Sensitive Data: Includes personally identifiable information (PII), protected health information (PHI),
and financial data.
3. COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENTS
All AI review activities will comply with relevant laws, including GDPR, PIPEDA, and sector-specific
rules. Legal and compliance teams must conduct periodic reviews to ensure ongoing adherence.
4. DATA PRIVACY & SECURITY
Strict access control protocols must be in place. All sensitive documents must be encrypted in transit
and at rest. Logs of tool usage must be reviewed quarterly to ensure proper handling.
5. REVIEW PROCESS & QUALITY ASSURANCE
Documents are processed by the AI review tool following established workflows. Accuracy metrics,
false positive/negative rates, and sampling audits must be maintained and reviewed regularly.
6. BIAS DETECTION & MITIGATION
The system must be tested for potential biases across protected attributes. If bias is detected,
mitigation actions such as retraining or parameter adjustments must be initiated and documented.
7. STAFF TRAINING & ACCESS CONTROL

## Page 2

All staff interacting with the system must complete mandatory training. Access rights must be
rolenbased and reviewed seminannually. All access must be logged.
8. INCIDENT & ERROR REPORTING
Errors, misclassifications, or security incidents must be reported immediately. An escalation path must
be followed, including investigation and remediation steps.
9. AUDITING & CONTINUOUS MONITORING
Quarterly audits will be conducted to validate tool performance, security, and compliance. Continuous
monitoring must track emerging risks and system changes.
10. POLICY REVIEW & AMENDMENT
The policy will be reviewed annually by the compliance officer or designated authority, with updates
approved and communicated organizationnwide.
