ACME Bank — Vendor Risk Policy (Excerpt)
Effective Date: 2025-01-01
Owner: Third Party Risk Management

1. Purpose
This policy defines minimum controls for onboarding and monitoring third-party vendors.

2. Requirements
MUST: All vendors handling customer PII must complete an annual security questionnaire.
MUST: Contracts must include a data breach notification clause within 72 hours.
SHOULD: Vendors should provide SOC 2 Type II reports when available.

3. Definitions
PII: Personally Identifiable Information.
SOC 2: Service Organization Control 2.
