# ACME Bank — Data Retention Standard (Excerpt)

Effective Date: 2025-03-01

Owner: Information Governance

## 1. Scope

This standard applies to customer and internal records stored in approved systems.

## 2. Requirements

MUST: Customer statements must be retained for at least 7 years.

MUST: Deletion requests must be logged and approved by the data owner.

SHOULD: Retention schedules should be reviewed annually.

## 3. Table — Record Classes

### Table 1

| Record Class | Minimum Retention | System of Record || --- | --- | --- || Customer Statements | 7 years | Core Banking || Security Logs | 1 year | SIEM || HR Files | 7 years | HRIS |
