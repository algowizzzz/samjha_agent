# Collateral Acceptance Policy Draft – Needs Improvement

## 1. Overview
The purpose of this draft policy is to outline how the lending desk handles collateral. It loosely aligns with internal credit practices but requires refinement to ensure full regulatory and governance alignment.

## 2. Scope
Applies broadly to lending activities in capital markets. More detail is needed on covered products, counterparties, and regional applicability. Exclusions are not clearly defined and require expansion.

## 3. Policy Requirements
- Collateral should usually be acceptable based on internal rules.
- Valuations are performed, but the methodology and frequency are not clearly stated.
- Haircuts apply, but schedules and exceptions are not listed.
- Legal review happens in most cases, but requirements need clarity.
- Requirements lack measurable criteria and traceability.

## 4. Roles & Responsibilities
Roles are partially defined:
- Lending Desk: Handles onboarding.
- Credit and Risk: Provide oversight.
Needs: clearer division of authority, escalation rights, and decision-making boundaries.

## 5. Operational Procedures
Some steps are mentioned but lack structure:
- Check collateral.
- Get valuation.
- Review exceptions.
Needs: reference to SOPs, standard workflows, and alignment with enterprise controls.
